### Added at the 2026-09-04 slice-3 review remediation

- **RESOLVED — owner-approved correction to the zone filter.** The coast-only filter dropped 144 of the 243 live precipitation rows, including `PRECIPITACIONES EN LA SIERRA` (78 rows, the page's most common precipitation title). The Río La Leche rises inside Ferreñafe province and SENAMHI gauges it at Puchaca in Incahuasi, also inside the province, so sierra rain is upstream of the city with hours of lead time. Highlands-only rainfall is flood-relevant again as an **early signal**: it may contribute to `prepare` and may not raise `imminent` on its own. `is_flood_relevant(phenomenon)` and `may_raise_imminent(phenomenon, zone)` are now two rules answering two questions. See design §5.1.1 and the new `risk-evaluation` requirement.
- **RESOLVED — the carried "discard audit cannot be surfaced" item was based on a wrong premise.** `Available` is a plain frozen dataclass, so `notes: tuple[str, ...] = ()` is purely additive, breaks no merged call site and keeps `mypy --strict` green. Discard reasons and a parse-anomaly summary now reach the CLI's `Notes` block and `--json`. See design §5.1.2.
- **RESOLVED — `COSTA SUR` residual risk is no longer a way to miss a warning.** With highlands-only rainfall kept, the zone rule can only limit severity, never remove a warning. The item stands only as a calibration question: whether `COSTA SUR` should reduce a warning's weight. Carried to change 3.
- **Open, carried to change 3 — `JsonFileAlertRepository.alerts` grows without bound.** Every `record_alert` rewrites the whole document, so both the file and the write cost grow forever. It is not urgent here: one send per six-hour cycle, only on an escalation, and the file is gitignored and disposable. **Proposed retention rule**: on write, keep only records whose `window.end` is within `dedup_lookback_hours * 2` of `now`, since `AlertPolicy` never queries further back than `now - dedup_lookback_hours` (clamped to the assessment window start) — doubling it leaves a wide margin for a long-duration aviso. Deliberately not implemented in this slice: it needs a clock in the repository, which the port does not carry, and change 3's DynamoDB adapter should use a TTL attribute rather than a rewrite, so the two adapters would not share the mechanism anyway.
- **RESOLVED — the third copy of the window-start query is gone.** `tests/support/fakes.FakeAlertRepository` now composes `InMemoryAlertRepository` and records calls around it, and the port-contract test in `tests/unit/adapters/test_local_repositories.py` runs against it too. The copy had already diverged: `clear_active_outage` ignored `city_slug`.
